Tropical nights: days in July–September with a daily minimum temperature of 25 °C or higher, interpolated from JMA weather stations, corrected with 100 m land use and averaged over population points in each area. Insufficient rest from sleep: proportion answering “no” to “Do you get enough rest from sleep?” in specific health checkups (NDB Open Data), standardized to the national sex and age structure of fiscal year 2022. Change in 2023: tropical nights in 2023 minus the area’s 2018–2022 mean. Modeled difference: the difference in predicted reports of insufficient rest from sleep per 1,000 respondents when the 2023 tropical nights are replaced with that mean (aggregated binomial model). The values are area averages, not values for single homes or persons.
Source: D. Matsushita, “Tropical nights and insufficient rest from sleep: a nationwide panel study of 335 secondary medical care areas in Japan, 2018–2023” (submitted). Data and code: github.com/m138/japan-hotnight-sleep. Map lines delineate study areas and do not necessarily depict accepted national boundaries.
